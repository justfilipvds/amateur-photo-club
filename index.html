Lviv Aperture Club is forty-some people with cameras — phones, film, DSLRs — who walk the city together, trade honest critique, and print what they make. No portfolio required to join, just curiosity.

01 / About the club

Built by members, for members

We started in 2019 as five neighbours swapping photos in a group chat. Today we're a club of roughly 48 active members, aged 17 to 71, who meet every Thursday evening and one Saturday a month for a longer shoot outside the city.

Nobody here shoots for a living. That's the point. We critique kindly, share settings freely, and believe a good photograph can come from a phone in a trolleybus just as easily as a tripod on a hill.

02 / What we do

A rhythm that fits around real life

01

Thursday walks

A two-hour walk through a different district each week — Pidzamche one week, the Opera quarter the next. Open to all, no RSVP needed, meet at the listed corner at 19:00.

02

Monthly critique night

Bring three photos, printed or on a laptop. We project them and talk through composition, light, and editing choices — no grading, just useful notes from people who shoot too.

03

Saturday field trips

One longer outing a month beyond the city — Vysokyi Zamok at sunrise, the Carpathian foothills in autumn, a night shoot on the market square. Carpooling arranged in the group chat.

04

Annual exhibition

Each December we print and hang a show of member work in a local café or gallery space. Everyone who shows up through the year gets a frame on the wall.

“You don't need a good camera. You need to show up on Thursday and look closely at something.”

05 / Questions

Before your first Thursday

Still unsure? Message us before showing up and someone will walk you through what to bring.

Contact the club
Do I need a proper camera?

No. About a third of us shoot primarily on phones. Bring whatever you have — the walks are about seeing, not gear.

Is there a membership fee?

No fee to join or attend walks. We split print costs for the annual exhibition among whoever is submitting that year.

What skill level is this for?

All levels. We have members who picked up a camera this year and members who have shot for two decades. Critique nights are kind, not competitive.

Where do Thursday walks meet?

The meeting point changes weekly with the neighbourhood. It's posted in the group chat every Monday and on this page by Wednesday.

Can I bring a friend who isn't into photography?

Of course. Plenty of people join a walk first and pick up a camera later.

Do you shoot in winter?

Year-round, rain or snow. We dress for it and shorten the route when it's below −10 °C.
